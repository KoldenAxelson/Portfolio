Live · cel shading and rim light, one pixel at a time
Light steps

The formulas are the video's: brightness is the dot product of the surface normal and the light direction; cel shading rounds it up to a fixed number of steps (multiply, ceil, divide); the rim is one minus the dot of normal and view, times the light, stepped the same way and squared to sharpen it. The sphere is mine, drawn at 64 pixels and scaled up without smoothing.
