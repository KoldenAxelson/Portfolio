Live · wind from a scrolling noise texture

The video's recipe: one shared noise texture, sampled at each blade's position in the world and scrolled over time, remapped to −1…1 so blades bend both ways, and masked so only the top moves. Untick the first box and every blade reads the same noise value; untick the second and whole blades slide instead of bending. Speed and strength stand in for the video's shader globals.
