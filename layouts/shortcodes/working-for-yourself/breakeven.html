Live · break-even, and why the gap between price and cost decides it
Each unit contributes $7.00 toward fixed costs. Break-even: 300 units a month.
Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin, where contribution margin = price − variable cost. The numbers are illustrative. Drag the variable cost toward the price and watch the break-even run away, which is the video's warning made literal.
