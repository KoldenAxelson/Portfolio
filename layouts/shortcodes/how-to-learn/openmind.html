Live · the same passage, read two ways
The passage is rigged, but rigged the way real arguments are: every objection is answered by a sentence further down. With judgement running, the objection ends your attention on the sentence that provoked it, so you never reach the answer — and the argument you end up disagreeing with is one you assembled yourself.
