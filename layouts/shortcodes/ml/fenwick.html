Invented counts, numbered 1 to 16, sit in the bottom row. Each bar above stores the total of the positions it spans, ending where it's stored, with a length equal to the biggest power of two dividing that position. A read adds bars jumping left, and an add changes the bars covering it, jumping right: highlighted bars are read, striped ones changed, shaded counts show what's covered. With 16 counts, a read adds at most 4 bars and an add changes at most 5.
