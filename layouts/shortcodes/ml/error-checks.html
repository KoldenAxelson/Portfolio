The message is the word HELLO, five bytes of 8 bits, and bits that changed are striped. Each check is worked out once on what was sent and again on what arrived; if the two differ, the check caught the damage. The sum keeps only its last 8 bits, so HELLO's 372 shows as 116. A check that missed would let the damaged word through as if it were fine.
