The fast Walsh–Hadamard transform on 8 made-up numbers, worked out for real. Each stage pairs every number with the one 1, 2, then 4 places along: the upper box of each pair gets a + b, the lower one gets a − b. The outputs come out in the standard (Sylvester) order, the one scipy.linalg.hadamard uses. Picking a number outlines everything it was made from and shades everything that reuses it.
