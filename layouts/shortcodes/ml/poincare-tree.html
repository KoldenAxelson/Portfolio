Both layouts are computed for this tree. "Worst distortion" takes each pair of nodes' layout distance over their tree distance and divides the largest ratio by the smallest, so 1 is perfect; the flat figure is for this layout, not the best flat one. Very short edges crowd the disk, which can then do worse than flat with 3 children per node. Long edges push the leaves to the rim, where telling them apart takes more digits (the gap is measured on a disk of radius 1).
