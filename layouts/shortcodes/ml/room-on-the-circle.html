"Room" counts nodes spaced one unit apart on the circle at that level's distance r from the root: 2πr in flat space and 2π sinh r in hyperbolic space (curvature −1), where r is the level times the distance between levels. Lines stop at 100 million, the top of the chart. Hyperbolic room keeps up for good once e to the power of the distance between levels is at least the children per node; flat room never keeps up for long.
