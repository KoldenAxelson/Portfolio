The numbers are invented, and every total is computed from them. D is the table cell at the rectangle's bottom-right corner, B the one just above its top-right corner, C the one just left of its bottom-left corner, and A the one diagonally above and to the left; a corner off the grid counts as 0. The steps show how many times each cell has been counted: only the cells inside the rectangle end at exactly once.
