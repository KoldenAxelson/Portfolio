Click a position (or use the arrow keys) to follow it. Try multiplying by an even number.
Real arithmetic on small clocks: 8 to 64 positions instead of the 4,294,967,296 a 32-bit number has, which behave the same way. Solid dots are reached by one arrow, red dots by more (the ×2 beside one says how many) and hollow dots by none; a position that stays put has no arrow. Dashed arrows are the undo, pointing back.
