The Bagii Atoll is a broken ring of fourteen islets off
[Kretavia](/dagea/regions/kretavia/)'s east coast, at the eastern end of the
[Sea of Coin](/dagea/regions/sea-of-coin/). It is the second home of the Bagii and
the only place where Feldian and Kretavian merchants reliably do business face to
face. There is no seat of government. The market moves with the season, and both
sides prefer it that way.

## The ring

The atoll is the last of the mountain chain that walls off the Wastes. The range
runs south the length of the world, drops into the Sea of Coin, and surfaces one
final time here as fourteen peaks with their heads barely above water. Kirchberg
scholars worked this out from the rock a century ago and were pleased with
themselves; the Bagii had been saying it for longer than that.

The islets are low, hot and bare. Nothing grows on them worth eating. There is no
spring on the atoll and no timber, so drinking water is caught in cisterns, sold by
the barrel, and rationed openly in a dry year. Buildings are coral rag, mortar and
imported wood. The lagoon inside the ring is broad and calm enough to hold a great
many hulls, and one deep channel — the Gate, on the north-west arc — is the only
way in for a loaded ship. A Bagii pilot takes every vessel through it.

## The market

The fair runs from the end of the winter gales until the autumn turn, and the
lagoon in high season holds more tonnage than most Feldian ports see in a year.
Warehouses on the two largest islets are leased by the month. Berths are assigned
by the Bagii harbor-keepers and lost the same way.

The customs are short and enforced without much discussion. No flags. Weapons stay
aboard, which applies to Kretavic escort troops and to Feldian hired swords alike.
Disputes go to a panel of Bagii who hear both sides and rule the same afternoon,
and a merchant who ignores a ruling does not get a berth next season — a penalty
that sounds mild until you price the alternative. Weights and measures are checked
against a set of scales the Bagii keep and both sides have agreed to trust. The
agreement is older than most of the houses trading under it.

## Goods

North to south: grain from [Kornfeld](/dagea/regions/kornfeld/), wine from
[Kronstadt](/dagea/regions/kronstadt/), furs and timber from
[Waldheim](/dagea/regions/waldheim/), iron and coal from
[Steinmark](/dagea/regions/steinmark/), salt fish from
[Seehaven](/dagea/regions/seehaven/). South to north: Kretavic metalwork and
textiles, dates and citrus, dyes, spice, and finished goods a Feldian workshop
cannot match. Very little is consumed here. The atoll is a place things pass
through, and it takes its cut on the way.

Most cargo now moves on credit rather than coin. Letters drawn in Kronstadt are
honored on the atoll, and the Bagii houses that clear them have grown wealthy
doing nothing but standing between two parties who dislike each other.

## Getting there

From the [Bagii Isles](/dagea/regions/bagii-isles/) the crossing takes ten days in
fair weather, and the two Bagii settlements pass ships, crews and news between them
constantly. From Feldia the atoll route avoids the Seehaven canal, which is slower
and dearer than the direct passage and much less likely to end in an argument with
a Kretavic patrol.

## What merchants are talking about

Traffic in iron and weapons has climbed sharply over the past two years. Consignees
are vague, shipments arrive broken into small lots under several names, and payment
comes through instruments complicated enough that nobody at the quayside can say
who is buying. Berth rents on the eastern islets have doubled. Kretavic escort
detachments have grown, and they now stay through the whole season rather than
sailing home with their convoys. The Bagii have made no comment about any of it,
which the older factors say is the Bagii making a comment.
