East of [Feldia](/dagea/regions/feldia/), past the Spine, the maps stop being maps and
start being guesses. The Wastes are the country on the far side of that mountain wall.
Almost nothing about them is established beyond doubt, and this article will not pretend
otherwise.

## What is known

The Spine runs from the northern coast down to the south-east, and it is unbroken enough
that crossing it is a matter of a handful of passes, all of them high, most of them shut
by snow for the greater part of the year. [Steinmark](/dagea/regions/steinmark/) holds
the Cold Gate at the northern end and keeps a garrison on it.
[Eisenmark](/dagea/regions/eisenmark/) holds what lies further south and does not discuss
its arrangements. Past the passes the ground falls away into broken, dry, stony country
that supports no farming anyone has managed and no settlement anyone can name.

Nothing is exported from the Wastes. No duchy claims territory there. No road goes in
that was built rather than worn.

## What is said

Accounts exist. They are few, they disagree, and the people who bring them back are
rarely in a condition to be cross-examined. Herders on the Steinmark side speak of
weather that arrives from the east against the wind, and of animals refusing a slope for
a season and then walking it again. Old soldiers who served on the passes will tell you they never saw anything, and
say it in the tone of men who would rather not be asked twice.

Feldian clergy have their own account, in which the Wastes are less a place than a
consequence — the visible mark of something the world has not recovered from — and the
learned in [Kirchberg](/dagea/regions/kirchberg/) have written a good deal on the
subject without settling it. The scholarly consensus, if it can be called that, is that
the country is barren for ordinary reasons, that the stories are what frightened people
at the edge of a map produce, and that the matter does not warrant an expedition.

Nobody has funded an expedition.

## Why it matters

Feldia sleeps easily about its eastern frontier for one reason, which is the Spine.
Whatever is or is not out there, the mountains are between. That has been sufficient for
as long as the realm has existed, and it is a comfort that gets repeated a little more
often, and a little more deliberately, than it used to be.
