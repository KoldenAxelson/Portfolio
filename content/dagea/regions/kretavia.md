Kretavia is the desert nation on the southern shore of the [Sea of Coin](/dagea/regions/sea-of-coin/),
and the only power in the charted world that rivals [Feldia](/dagea/regions/feldia/)
for age, reach, and certainty about itself. It is governed by a council drawn from
religious and military authority, in roughly that order of precedence. Its faith is
older than Feldia's and considers itself the source of it.

## The land

Desert dominates, and everything else follows from that. Water decides where people
live: cities cluster at oases and along the few river systems, and the distance
between them is measured in the water a party can carry. The climate is not
dramatic so much as unforgiving. It kills the unprepared quietly and in ordinary
weather.

Caravan crews will tell you the deep desert has places where time does not keep.
The stories are consistent enough to be worth repeating and vague enough to be
worth doubting: a rider who arrives three days before he set out, a camp that burns
down every night and is whole every morning, a man who came back speaking of forty
years and had not aged a season. Kretavic scholars argue about whether these are
divine, diabolical, or simply a flaw in the country — a scar in the world that
never healed properly. The argument is old and nobody has won it. Guides charge
more to cross the stretches where the stories cluster, and they do not linger.

## People and faith

Kretavic society is hierarchical and devout, and the two are the same fact. Standing
comes from faith, from birth, and from martial skill, in that order. The Kretavic
Faith teaches that the unconverted live in darkness and that bringing them out of it
is the highest work a life can do — which produces missionaries, and produces them
armed. A Kretavic trade delegation carries more soldiers than the goods require.
Whether that is escort or reconnaissance depends on who you ask, and both answers
have evidence.

The language is Kretivic. It is spoken well beyond Kretavia's borders, mostly along
the trade routes and mostly badly.

## Trade and craft

What the desert does not provide, Kretavia buys, and it buys a great deal. Farming
is confined to irrigated ground near water — dates, grain, vegetables. Against that,
Kretavic metalwork and textiles are as fine as anything made in the north, and the
country maintains a standing army in peacetime, which few of its neighbors can
afford to do.

Most Feldian merchants never see the mainland. Trade happens at the
[Bagii Atoll](/dagea/regions/bagii-atoll/) off the eastern coast, where northern and
southern ships meet, settle, and go home.

## Feldia

Relations are cold and entirely transactional. The two nations trade because both
profit and trust each other not at all — a state of affairs with three centuries of
history behind it and no obvious end. Kretavic raiding on the Feldian coast around
[Seehaven](/dagea/regions/seehaven/) is regular, destructive, and has made that
duchy permanently hostile to anything sailing up from the south.

More recently, and more quietly, Kretavic trade with the Feldian duchy of
[Eisenmark](/dagea/regions/eisenmark/) has grown a great deal, and diplomatic
contact between them has become routine. Neither party has explained why. In a
relationship this old and this cold, that is the kind of thing merchants notice and
talk about.
