Kronstadt is the capital of [Feldia](/dagea/regions/feldia/) and the largest trading
city in the north — loud, crowded, and permanently mid-argument. It is the seat of
König Aldric, who holds the title of High King. Few dukes would concede that his
authority runs deeper than his persuasion, and few merchants in Kronstadt care either
way, so long as the river stays open.

## The land

The city stands where the Kron meets the Ellwater, and everything about it follows from
that. Warehouses run down to the waterfront on both banks; seagoing ships moor at the
lower quays and lighters shuttle cargo up past the bridges. Three great stone bridges
carry the city across itself, and each has its own reputation — one for the toll, one
for the beggars, one for the view.

West of the city the ground rises into the Feldian Wine Country: rolling hills,
vineyards, and the estates of nobles who prefer country air to court air but keep town
houses for the season anyway. Beyond that the Kron reaches the west coast. North across
the river lies [Waldheim](/dagea/regions/waldheim/), which the Kron divides from
Kronstadt neatly enough that both duchies have run out of excuses to dispute it.

The climate is temperate and rains a great deal. Winters can turn hard but never
approach the severity of the eastern duchies. The soil around the city carries market
gardens close enough that the greens on a Kronstadt table were in the ground that
morning.

## The city

Kronstadt holds roughly eighty thousand people. It is dense and vertical, built of pale
stone under slate and tile, with lanes that were laid out for handcarts and now carry
wagons. The High King's palace sits at the center — not ostentatious, but very large and
placed where it can see most approaches. The merchant quarter owns the waterfront. The
banking houses line the northern ridge, above the flood line and above everybody else.
Workshops, guildhalls and tenements fill the rest.

The city smells of smoke, horse, river and money.

Away from the walls, the wine country runs to its own rhythm. [Aurath](/dagea/regions/aurath/)
produces the duchy's finest reds and is governed by its mayor with a seriousness the
size of the place does not warrant. [Mittenfeld](/dagea/regions/mittenfeld/) is the
market town where wine is traded, assessed, and argued over by men who can name a
vintage from half a glass. Dozens of smaller villages sit under a baron or an appointed
mayor, serving the vineyards and the estates.

## People

Kronstadters are merchants first and subjects second. Standing is measured in coin and
connections; bloodline matters chiefly as a thing to be leveraged well. The city pulls
in the ambitious from every duchy — younger sons, craftsmen chasing the largest market,
soldiers who want steady pay — and the result is a place where conversation moves fast
and trust is extended slowly and in writing.

The wine country breeds a different sort. Landed gentry and prosperous farmers, wealth
counted in vineyards and reputation rather than quarterly returns, civic-minded and
comfortably distant from the daily knife-work of the capital.

The Church here is wealthy and political. The Bishop of Kronstadt advises the High King
regularly, and the two men are careful never to look as though either serves the other.

## Trade and craft

Kronstadt's real wealth is not in what it makes but in being indispensable to everyone
else's commerce. Goods come in from every duchy and down the rivers, and the banking
houses handle the credit, the currency, and the letters that make long-distance trade
possible at all. Access to the court is bought and sold here as openly as cloth.

Wine is the duchy's own product and its luxury export — one of the few Feldian goods the
wealthier southern nations actively want. Beyond that the city makes fine cloth, jewellery
and luxury work: things priced for craftsmanship rather than raw material. The guilds are
strong, competitive, and quick to litigate. If something can be made better here than
elsewhere in Feldia, somebody in Kronstadt is already trying.

The Grand Market is the oldest in the city and the place where information trades as
freely as goods. The merchant network known as the Coin keeps its house here. The Three
Rivers Library is a university of a practical sort — accounting, navigation, languages,
history — and is looked down upon by [Kirchberg](/dagea/regions/kirchberg/), which it
affects not to notice.

## History

Kronstadt has been the center of Feldian power for seven generations. The title of High
King is younger than that: a compromise between ambitious dukes and the plain need for
somebody to coordinate them. Before it existed, Kronstadt simply held first place by
being richest, and its rulers behaved much as they do now.

The council of dukes still meets at the palace three times yearly. What is decided there
is binding. What happens afterward is negotiated.
