The Old Wood covers the western arm of [Kretavia](/dagea/regions/kretavia/), where the
land turns south from the coast of [Sahalim](/dagea/regions/sahalim/) and climbs. It is
mountain forest, wet on the seaward side and cold in winter, and it is the only part of
the country with timber worth the name. In a nation that burns charcoal hauled four
days across the desert, that makes this coast quietly essential.

## The land

The mountains come down to the water steeply and leave almost no harbor. Adhran is the
one sheltered anchorage of any size, and it is a hard beat to reach in a westerly. Most
of what the Wood produces therefore goes the other way: cut on the slopes, dragged to
the headwaters, and floated down the Green Water to Kretivat, where it is rafted on to
the coast. The timber road and the river are the same road.

The forest is old in a way that people mention unprompted. Stands of it have never been
cut, the trunks run to sizes a Feldian shipwright would not believe, and the ground
under them takes a lantern to read at noon.

## Work

Timber and resin. The resin is the more valuable of the two by weight — it is burned in
the temples of [the Kharsa](/dagea/regions/the-kharsa/), boiled into varnish for hulls
in Tannurah, and hardens over years into a clear amber that goes north as a luxury.
Charcoal burners work the lower slopes for the desert trade. Cutting rights are granted
by the temple and taken seriously: a felled tree is paid for with a planted one, the
count is recorded, and a woodsman who falsifies it loses his rights for life.

Nobody cuts the oldest stands. There is no written prohibition. There does not appear to
have ever been one.

## Custom

The faith here is as strict as anywhere in Kretavia and the habits underneath it are
older, which the temples have long since stopped making an issue of. Things are left at
the treeline. Men going up for a season's cutting do not explain themselves to
outsiders and are not asked to twice.

## The island

Everyone on this coast knows that the land offshore was once part of it. The charts show
the bite; the folk story says the Wood lost a limb and grew over the wound. What is out
there now is [Jrakvia](/dagea/regions/jrakvia/), which does not trade, does not send
word, and does not receive visitors by any arrangement anyone has been able to make.
Boats that go west from Adhran come back, or they do not come back here.

Scholars in the oasis cities occasionally argue about whether the forest on the island
is the same forest. It is not a question anyone has been in a position to answer.
