Dagea is a world I drew on paper and then wrote down. The map is
[a globe you can spin](/misc/dagea/); this is everything the map does not say.

These pages are what a literate person inside the world could tell you — geography,
trade, faith, custom, and the things travelers claim. They are not the campaign.
