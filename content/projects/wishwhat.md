## Problem

Ask someone what they want for their birthday and you get "Legos". Buy them
Legos and so do three other people. Wish lists exist, but they live in
different apps, the grandparents never install any of them, and the person
with the list can usually see what's been bought, which spoils the surprise.

WishWhat puts the list, the friends and the party in one app. You add exactly
what you want, by link or by hand. Friends mark what they've bought, and
everyone sees it except you. Invites go by text message, so a grandparent can
open a list without signing up for anything first.

## What we built

Phil Honeycutt was the designer and art director; I was the engineer. We ran
it from a Discord server with missions, time logs and a push-up penalty for
missed check-ins. Between October 2024 and March 2025 Phil designed most of
the app in Figma: the sitemap, a design system, onboarding, a five-step
profile setup that hands your friends gift ideas, and the events screens. I
had an art-less iOS app building in Xcode in the first week, then moved the
plan to Flutter so iOS and Android could ship together.

## Why it stopped

Christmas 2024 slipped to spring, and spring slipped too. We both had full
lives outside the app, and in early 2025 my attention moved to
[GrowGo](/projects/growgo).

## Current state

It's on hold. We took the Discord server down in October 2026 and
saved everything worth keeping (features, screens, the money model, the plan)
into a project pitch for the day we pick it back up. It lives on this site
behind a numeric keypad at [/misc/wishwhat](/misc/wishwhat). If you'd like to
see it, [ask me for the code](mailto:KonradWright@Protonmail.com).
