## Problem

A YouTube automotive studio wanted a home for the brand and a way to sell
shirts and hats. The default answer is Shopify — and for a merch line this
size, Shopify is a monthly bill, a transaction cut, and an admin surface
nobody at a garage wants to think about.

## Approach

Astro on Cloudflare Pages, built from scratch. The only recurring cost is the
domain — twelve dollars a year, in perpetuity. The studio got every tool they
need and none they don't: no plugin updates, no platform dashboard, no
subscription to remember to cancel.

## Outcome

Live at [bighammergarage.com](https://bighammergarage.com/). A couple dozen
shirts and hats sold as of August 2026, at effectively zero overhead — the
merch margin is the merch margin.
