## How it plays

The plan is two halves: stop my opponent from ever landing an attack, then win on effect damage while they're stuck.

### The lock

Gravity Bind and Level Limit - Area B keep anything big from swinging. Nightmare's Steelcage and Swords of Revealing Light buy turns outright, and Mask of the Accursed shuts down whatever slips through. Marshmallon, Mecha-Dog Marron and Shiba-Warrior Taro hold the field, while Mirror Force, Magic Cylinder and Dimension Wall punish anyone who attacks anyway.

### The burn

Des Koala and Secret Barrel count my opponent's cards against them, and Ceasefire flips every face-down monster for damage. Just Desserts, Stealth Bird and Ebon Magician Curran chip away every turn the lock holds, Lava Golem and Ojama Trio clog their side of the field, and Wave-Motion Cannon plus Chain Strike close it out once the game has dragged on long enough.
