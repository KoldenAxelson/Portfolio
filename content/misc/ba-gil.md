Ba-Gil is a language I'm building. Its name is its own first lesson: **Ba**
means future, **Gil** means speak, so Ba-Gil is *future-speak*. The rules I'm
holding it to: completely systematic with no exceptions, only sounds any human
can make, nothing in conversation longer than five syllables, and able to name
things that haven't been invented yet.

It's very much in progress, and I'm building it in the open. The hatched cells
below are the parts that don't exist yet.

## The letters {#letters}

Sixteen letters, and the alphabet's order spells out its own pieces:
*ba-ked-gil-mon-pus-üt*. Each letter is also a digit, which comes back in
[Numbers](#numbers).

## Three shapes {#shapes}

Every piece of Ba-Gil has one of three shapes. Consonant, vowel, consonant is a
root word. Drop the last consonant and it's a prefix; drop the first and it's a
suffix. A piece's shape tells you where it belongs in a word.

{{< ba-gil/shapes >}}

## Frames {#frames}

Words come in families. Hold the consonants still and change only the vowel, and
you walk along a scale in alphabet order: K_L runs through the colours in
wavelength order, K_D runs from a single letter up to a whole book. Learn one row
and you can guess the next. The first consonant picks the family, and there are
ten families, one per consonant.

{{< ba-gil/frames >}}

## Inversion {#inversion}

This is the heart of it. Prefixes and suffixes are mirror-image shapes, so a
piece sitting in the wrong position is easy to spot, and the language reads it as
an inversion. Future, person, past is someone spanning all of time: immortal.
Swap the ends and you get past, person, future, someone moving *through* time:
mortal.

The same mirror runs through the scales. Ma- to Mu- say how wrong something is;
-Am to -Um, the same pieces turned around, say how much better.

{{< ba-gil/perversion >}}

## Building words {#words}

Words stack like German, piece after piece. Body parts describe how things work
(a window is a house's eye), and the family frame works on anything (a seed is a
plant's child). Type one in, or pick an example.

{{< ba-gil/builder >}}

## Numbers {#numbers}

Ba-Gil counts in base 16, and the digits are the alphabet: b is 0, a is 1, all
the way to t at 15. A # marks a number, and # is said *chi*: the ch sound exists
only to mark numbers, so it never turns up in an ordinary word. Each digit is said
as chi plus its letter.
